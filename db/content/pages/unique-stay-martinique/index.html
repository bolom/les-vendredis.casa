Why choose this unique stay in Martinique?

A simple A-frame, a living garden, and the southern coast within easy reach.

A real A-frame

The A-frame was built by hand, in wood, on family land. Loft bed under the gable, equipped kitchen under the covered préau, outdoor shower in the garden, hot water since May 2026.

A private tropical garden

600m² for you, designed and planted by Anaïs: mango, guava, bougainvillea, passion fruit, rocks, and planted paths. When fruit falls during your stay, it is yours.

Sainte-Luce, on the nature side

You are in the hills of southern Martinique, a few minutes from the beaches of Sainte-Luce and close to Les Roches Gravées de Montravail. Sleep quietly, then drive down to the coast.

The comfort of outside

Here, luxury is in the air, space, shade, fruit, solar shower, and meals under the préau. Les Vendredis is a unique stay in Martinique for slowing down, reading in the hammock, cooking outside, and living in the garden with your pet.

Our place, when we're away

Les Vendredis wasn't designed as a tourism product. It's a family home in Sainte-Luce: we garden here, build here, eat outside here, and leave traces of daily life.

A stay between spaces

The stay happens as much in the A-frame as under the préau, in the fruit garden, in the hammocks, under the solar shower, and along the planted paths. You live the land, not just a room.

For guests who love lived-in places

Les Vendredis suits travellers who prefer the open air, the garden, the slow pace of a real home over the polish of a rental.

Take it as it is

What it actually is.

The facts, not the adjectives.

Is this a unique stay in Martinique?

Yes. Les Vendredis is a wooden A-frame built by hand in Sainte-Luce, with a loft bed, outdoor kitchen, garden shower, and 600m² of private tropical garden. It's a family home: simple, open, personal.

Where is the A-frame?

In Sainte-Luce, on the south coast, in the hills and a few minutes from the beaches by car. Les Roches Gravées de Montravail are nearby — one of three known pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

How many guests can stay?

The A-frame sleeps two adults, one child, and their pet. Parking is on site, in front of the land.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Les Vendredis is properly pet friendly. See our dedicated pet friendly page for details. The private garden gives space to walk, sniff, watch, sleep in the shade, and share the stay with you.

The comfort we choose.

Simple choices, coherent with outdoor living.

What kind of comfort should I expect?

Simple, very outdoor comfort: loft bed, kitchen under the préau, solar shower, hammocks, tropical garden, quiet, and horizon. The stay is made for open air and slowing down.

How does the A-frame close?

The A-frame is open by design — built to live with the garden rather than shut against it. We welcome you, we show you around, and we trust the place to be looked after the way it always has been.

Is there staff on site?

We welcome you, show you around, then the place is yours. The feel is autonomous and quiet — no hotel service, no permanent presence.

How do I book?

You can book on Airbnb or write to us directly on WhatsApp to check a date, ask a question, or prepare a stay with your pet.
