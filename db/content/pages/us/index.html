People & animals

01

Anaïs

Designer and project lead

Says no first, then goes ahead. Conceived the whole property and led its construction. Turned bare red earth into a cabin, then into an orchard. Grumbles often. Changes her mind, slowly but surely. Generous. Holds things together with Bolo despite the friction.

02

Bolo

Software engineer; CTO (ScionX)

Programmer by trade, labourer by alliance. Runs the brush cutter on weekends and lends a hand at planting time. Author of a great many ideas and a more modest number of completed projects.

03

Léon

The son

Five years old when the first stone was laid. Grew up at exactly the pace of the build. Both were finished at the same time.

04

Samsam

The dog

Taken in on a Saturday, seven years ago — hence the name. Anaïs had reservations at first, since entirely dropped.

05

Dimdim

Rooster; no official title

Banished from the upper yard, set off alone down the hill. Attached himself to the dog Samsam, whom he has escorted at a regulation distance ever since. Lives in the breadfruit tree. Has expressed no regrets to date.

06

Nox

The assistant; never on site

Artificial intelligence in the service of the house. Handles correspondence and screens while the others handle the land. Proposes, never commands. Signs invariably as Nox.

The cabin

Type : A-frame
Built : by hand
Construction : 2022–2024
Location : hills of Sainte-Luce
Land : Anaïs's

An A-frame cabin, built by hand in the hills of Sainte-Luce. Anaïs designed it and led the build. Two years of construction, with the help of a carpenter and friends who came to lend a hand.

Outside: a covered terrace, a solar shower, the garden all around. No air conditioning — the height of the gable and the trade winds are enough for natural ventilation.

Fully equipped kitchen, open to the garden. Sheets and towels provided. A living, imperfect house.

The garden & its residents

The surroundings

Location

In the hills of Sainte-Luce. The dry forest of the south, not the beach. A quiet, rural, established neighbourhood.

Montravail Forest

Walkable from the cabin. Old mahogany trees, plenty of shade, marked trails. Very quiet.

Roches Gravées

About twenty minutes on foot. Amerindian faces carved into andesite. Listed as a historic monument since 1996.

The village

Sainte-Luce, a few kilometres away. Line 22 connects the Montravail stop (5 min walk) to the village in 20 min for €1.50. The beach at Sainte-Luce is reachable by bus; a car is useful for exploring further afield.

The cabin was built between 2022 and 2024.

It opens to guests when the family is not there.
