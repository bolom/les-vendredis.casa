A stay with a pet, really.

Not just a checkbox on a booking platform.

A garden to live in

The private tropical garden is the main room of the stay: mango trees, guava, planted paths, shade, rocks, and calm corners. Your pet can live outside with you, not wait in a room.

A place made for the outdoors

The A-frame opens to the garden, the kitchen is under the préau, the shower is outside, the hammocks too. This is what makes Les Vendredis different from a standard rental.

A simple rhythm

Wake up early, enjoy the cool hours, retreat to the shade when the heat rises. The stay suits pets used to travelling, to being close, and to staying near you.

Unique accommodation in Martinique

If you are looking for a unique stay in Martinique that welcomes pets without losing the spirit of the place, this is exactly what Les Vendredis is for.

What to plan for.

So the stay stays simple for everyone.

A car

It is essential. Les Vendredis is in the hills of Sainte-Luce, far from public transport. For beaches, groceries, the vet, or walks, you will need to be self-sufficient.

The heat

Martinique is hot, even in the hills. Water, bowl, shady spots, and calm hours matter more than anything. The garden helps, but you need to respect your pet's rhythm.

Beaches and trails

In Martinique, pets are not welcome everywhere. Some beaches, official trails, and public spaces do not allow them. We will tell you what is realistic around Sainte-Luce for your pet.

Respecting the place

Les Vendredis is our family home. We welcome pets because we live with them here ourselves. We simply ask that you respect the garden, the neighbours, the open spaces, and the local animals.

Frequently asked questions.

Before booking with your pet.

Are dogs welcome?

Yes. Dogs are welcome if the stay fits their temperament: open place, tropical garden, other animals in the area, and outdoor living.

Is the garden fenced?

The garden is private, but Les Vendredis remains an open place. If your pet tends to wander or chase things, plan a lead and stay attentive.

Can I leave my pet alone?

That is not the spirit of the place. The heat, the openness of the A-frame, and the outdoor lifestyle mean a pet should live the stay with you.

How do I book with a pet?

You can book on Airbnb, or write to us on WhatsApp beforehand to tell us which pet is coming with you. It is often simpler to check together that the stay works.
