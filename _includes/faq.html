Good to know.

Practical details for finding, booking, and staying at Les Vendredis.

Where is Les Vendredis located?

Les Vendredis is located in Sainte-Luce on the south coast of Martinique.

How close is Les Vendredis to the beach?

The cabin is three minutes from the Sainte-Luce coast on foot.

How do I book Les Vendredis?

You can book on Airbnb or Booking.com, or contact us on WhatsApp at +33 6 66 53 52 89.

How many guests can stay?

Les Vendredis sleeps two adults and one child.

Does Les Vendredis have a garden?

Yes. A private tropical garden with mango trees, bougainvillea, and planted paths.

What is Sainte-Luce?

Sainte-Luce is a village on the south coast of Martinique, a French Caribbean island.
