Good to know.

Practical details for finding, booking, and staying at Les Vendredis.

More than a unique stay in Martinique

Les Vendredis is a family place with a hand-built A-frame cabin, a covered préau, a private tropical garden, an outdoor shower, and a very outdoor way of living. See the unique stay →

Where is Les Vendredis located?

On route de Zaire in Sainte-Luce, in the hills of southern Martinique — surrounded by forest, away from the coast.

Do I need a car?

Yes, a car is essential. The hills are not served by public transport, and you'll want to move freely between the beaches of Sainte-Luce, Le Marin, and the rest of the south coast.

How do you sleep — is there air conditioning?

No air conditioning. The A-frame is tall under the gable, open to the garden, and crossed by the trade winds; you sleep in the cool of the evening and the sound of the trees.

Can we cook on site?

Yes. The kitchen opens onto the garden and is set up for simple cooking — the fruit, fish, and vegetables you'll find at the markets of Le Marin or Sainte-Luce.

What is there to do nearby?

Les Roches Gravées de Montravail — a classified historical monument with 14 Amerindian faces carved into andesite rock — are steps away, hidden in the Montravail forest. One of only three pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

How do I book Les Vendredis?

Book on Airbnb, or message us on WhatsApp.

How many guests can stay?

Les Vendredis sleeps two adults and one child.

Is there a garden?

Yes. A private tropical garden with mango trees, bougainvillea, and planted paths — it's a central part of the stay, not just scenery outside the cabin.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Les Vendredis is pet-friendly. The private garden gives animals space to roam freely.
