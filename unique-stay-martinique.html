What it actually is.

The facts, not the adjectives.

Is this a unique stay in Martinique?

Yes. Les Vendredis is a wooden A-frame built by hand in Sainte-Luce, with a loft bed, outdoor kitchen, garden shower, and 600m² of private tropical garden. It's a family home: simple, open, personal.

Where is the A-frame?

In Sainte-Luce, on the south coast, in the hills and a few minutes from the beaches by car. Les Roches Gravées de Montravail are nearby — one of three known pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

How many guests can stay?

The A-frame sleeps two adults, one child, and their pet. Parking is on site, in front of the land.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Les Vendredis is properly pet friendly. The private garden gives space to walk, sniff, watch, sleep in the shade, and share the stay with you.

The comfort we choose.

Simple choices, coherent with outdoor living.

What kind of comfort should I expect?

Simple, very outdoor comfort: loft bed, kitchen under the préau, solar shower, hammocks, tropical garden, quiet, and horizon. The stay is made for open air and slowing down.

How does the A-frame close?

The A-frame is open by design — built to live with the garden rather than shut against it. We welcome you, we show you around, and we trust the place to be looked after the way it always has been.

Is there staff on site?

We welcome you, show you around, then the place is yours. The feel is autonomous and quiet — no hotel service, no permanent presence.

How do I book?

You can book on Airbnb or write to us directly on WhatsApp to check a date, ask a question, or prepare a stay with your pet.
