Why choose this unique stay in Martinique?

A simple cabin, a living garden, and the southern coast within easy reach.

A real A-frame cabin

The cabin was built by hand, in wood, on family land. It is not a standard room dressed up as nature: loft bed under the gable, equipped kitchen in the open lean-to, outdoor shower in the garden, hot water since May 2026.

A private tropical garden

600m² for you, designed and planted by Anaïs: mango, guava, bougainvillea, passion fruit, rocks, and planted paths. When fruit falls during your stay, it is yours.

Sainte-Luce, on the nature side

You are in the hills of southern Martinique, a few minutes from the beaches of Sainte-Luce and close to Les Roches Gravées de Montravail. Sleep quietly, then drive down to the coast.

Not a spa resort

No pool, no jacuzzi, no AC, no TV, no wifi. Les Vendredis is a unique stay in Martinique for slowing down, reading in the hammock, cooking outside, and living in the garden with your pet.

What it actually is.

The facts, not the adjectives.

Is this a unique stay in Martinique?

Yes. Les Vendredis is a wooden A-frame cabin built by hand in Sainte-Luce, with a loft bed, outdoor kitchen, garden shower, and 600m² of private tropical garden. It is also unusual accommodation: simple, open, without TV, AC, or locks.

Where is the cabin?

In Sainte-Luce, on the south coast, in the hills and a few minutes from the beaches by car. Les Roches Gravées de Montravail are nearby — one of three known pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

How many guests can stay?

The cabin sleeps two adults, one child, and their pet. Parking is on site, in front of the land.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Les Vendredis is properly pet friendly. The private garden gives space to walk, sniff, watch, sleep in the shade, and share the stay with you.

What we don't do.

What we leave out, on purpose.

Is there a pool, jacuzzi, AC, or wifi?

No. No pool, no jacuzzi, no AC, no TV, no wifi. If you are looking for a spa resort, this is not the right address. If you want a unique stay in Martinique to slow down in a tropical garden, yes.

Does the cabin lock?

No. The cabin has no doors that close, no furniture with a key, no safe. Nobody has ever come up here to take anything. We'd like to keep it that way.

Is there staff on site?

No. No 24/7 reception, no room service. We show you around when you arrive and let you live.

How do I book?

You can book on Airbnb or write to us directly on WhatsApp to check a date, ask a question, or prepare a stay with your pet.
