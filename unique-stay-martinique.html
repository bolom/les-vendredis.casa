A simple cabin, a living garden, and the southern coast within easy reach.

What it actually is.

The facts, not the adjectives.

Is this a unique stay in Martinique?

Yes. Les Vendredis is a wooden A-frame cabin built by hand in Sainte-Luce, with a loft bed, outdoor kitchen, garden shower, and 600m² of private tropical garden. It is also a family domain: simple, open, personal, without TV, AC, or locks.

Where is the cabin?

In Sainte-Luce, on the south coast, in the hills and a few minutes from the beaches by car. Les Roches Gravées de Montravail are nearby — one of three known pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

How many guests can stay?

The cabin sleeps two adults, one child, and their pet. Parking is on site, in front of the land.

Are pets welcome?

Yes. Les Vendredis is properly pet friendly. The private garden gives space to walk, sniff, watch, sleep in the shade, and share the stay with you.

The comfort we choose.

Simple choices, coherent with outdoor living.

What kind of comfort should I expect?

Simple, very outdoor comfort: loft bed, kitchen under the lean-to, solar shower, hammocks, tropical garden, quiet, and horizon. No pool, jacuzzi, AC, TV, or wifi: the stay is made for open air and disconnection.

Does the cabin lock?

No. The cabin has no doors that close, no furniture with a key, no safe. Nobody has ever come up here to take anything. We'd like to keep it that way.

Is there staff on site?

We welcome you, show you around, then the domain is yours. The spirit is autonomous and quiet, without hotel service or permanent presence.

How do I book?

You can book on Airbnb or write to us directly on WhatsApp to check a date, ask a question, or prepare a stay with your pet.
