What it actually is.

The facts, not the adjectives.

The cabin

A wooden A-frame, built by hand between 2022 and 2026 on a slab we'd poured the year before. Bed in the loft under the gable. Kitchen set up in the open lean-to. Outdoor shower in the garden. Hot water since May 2026.

The garden

600m² private, designed by Anaïs, a landscape architect, and planted to produce year-round — mango, guava, bougainvillea, passion fruit. In season, you eat what falls.

The location

Sainte-Luce, on the south coast, a few minutes from the beaches. Les Roches Gravées de Montravail are nearby — one of three known pre-Columbian rock art sites in Martinique.

Who it's for

Two adults, a child, and their pet. Parking on site. Pet friendly, properly.

What we don't do.

What we leave out, on purpose.

No TV, no AC

No pool, no wifi either. You come for something else.

No staff

No 24/7 reception, no room service. We hand over the keys — well, we show you around — and we let you live.

No locks

The cabin has no doors that close, no furniture with a key, no safe. Nobody has ever come up here to take anything. We'd like to keep it that way.

Not a resort

If that's what you're looking for, this isn't it. If you're looking to slow down with your dog, read in a hammock, and hear Dimdim crow at five in the morning — this is the place.
