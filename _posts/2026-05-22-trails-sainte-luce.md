You don't need the car to run here. The cabin is 50 metres from the start of three trails that climb into the hills. Here are the routes we use most often.

## Montravail – Roches Gravées

The shortest one. Start from the cabin, head up Route de Zaire, then the forest path on the right. Twenty minutes walking, twelve running if you know the terrain. The trail is shaded, the gradient is gentle. You reach the Roches Gravées, pause in front of the faces, and head back the same way. Five kilometres round trip.

## Trace des Jésuites – Morne Gommier

Extend the Roches Gravées trail westwards. The Trace des Jésuites is an old communication path between the southern estates, laid out in the 17th century. It climbs in switchbacks through the forest up to Morne Gommier, at 290 metres above sea level. From up there, the view stretches across the entire south coast — from La Caravelle to Le Marin, and all the way to Saint Lucia on a clear day.

Allow two hours of walking for the full loop from the cabin. The path is well marked but lightly used. Good shoes recommended — the trail is rocky in places, and slippery in the wet season.

## Morne Aca – Rivière Pilote

For those who want to go further. Morne Aca tops out at 300 metres in the neighbouring commune of Rivière-Pilote. The trail follows the ridge between the two communes, alternating forest and farmland. Some technical sections with roots and rocks. Allow three hours round trip, with about 400 metres of cumulative elevation gain.

## Running here

Trail running in the Sainte-Luce hills is 80% shade, steady but rarely steep gradients, and terrain that varies between hardpack, stones, and roots. No need for GPS watches — the trails are linear, you won't get lost. In the dry season, the ground is fast. In the wet season, it's slick and slower, but the forest is at its best.

We start early — six in the morning, before the heat. Water is essential, even on short distances. A cap or buff, because some ridge sections are exposed to the sun.

## Our Sunday loop

Leave the cabin at six. Easy climb to the Roches Gravées, then push on to Morne Gommier. Pause at the top — the view, water, a mango if it's the season. Descend via the forest road, return along Route de Zaire. An hour and a half, 8 kilometres, and we're back for coffee.

If you're staying at Les Vendredis and you run, tell us. We'll show you the trails.
