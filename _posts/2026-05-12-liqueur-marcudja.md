Three breadfruits. Anaïs picked them yesterday — bright green, pebbled skin, each one the size of a child's head. The white latex still sticky on the stem.

Breadfruit (*Artocarpus altilis*) doesn't keep. So it gets eaten fast — boiled with salt and butter, or roasted whole in the coals. The tree will keep dropping them for another month.

![Three breadfruits on the concrete, garden in the background]({{ '/public/images/80031588110__4378D4D7-81C6-4001-82C9-AB8861EAAD01.webp' | relative_url }})

---

Anaïs started the liqueurs a few weeks ago. Two bottles on the shelf. One is **[Saint James Royal Blanc](https://rhum-saintjames.com/)** with marcudja seeds — already turning amber where the acid has given up its color. The other is **[Clément](https://www.rhum-clement.com/)**, clear and patient, with **marcudja** and **pomme liane** suspended in it. In Martinique it's marcudja — not passion fruit. Pomme liane (*Passiflora laurifolia*) is its cousin — softer, more floral, less acid.

She wrote **10 mai 2026** on the label. That's when they come off the shelf.

![Clément bottle infusing on the shelf]({{ '/public/images/80029108775__FFA43E1E-8799-484A-9285-F52C439C4A3E.webp' | relative_url }})

Both end up the same way — neat, on a Friday evening, when the work is done.

---

Catherine gave us her old BBQ. It had been in the rain too long — rust, flaking paint, bolts coming loose. Anaïs took it apart completely, scraped the oxidation, repainted the lid, tightened everything back down. Finished yesterday.

![The BBQ restored, ready to use]({{ '/public/images/80031235885__56F80068-FDAE-498C-A277-1FB60C9F72F5.webp' | relative_url }})

![Inside the BBQ bowl, clean and repainted]({{ '/public/images/80031238621__393D1747-3803-463A-ADD1-41A569562229.webp' | relative_url }})

Catherine has been around since before Léon was born. That's the kind of BBQ it is.

May gives things. You take care of what you receive.
