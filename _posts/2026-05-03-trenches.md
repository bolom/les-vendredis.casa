Hot water in the A-frame has been on the list for a while. This Sunday we finally started cutting the trenches to run the pipes from the main system across the garden and into the cabin.

Red soil, compact after the dry season. The pickaxe does the first pass, the spade clears it out. It's slow, hot work — the kind of task that looks simple on paper and takes the full day in practice.

Léon helped carry the pipe from the car to the site. He's getting good at the logistics.

The route goes under the stone wall Anaïs built, around the base of the mango tree (avoiding the roots, which Anaïs had very clear opinions about), and up into the cabin floor. About twelve metres in total.

We'll connect it properly next weekend. Then the A-frame will have everything it needs — hot shower, real kitchen, the whole thing.

Everything at Les Vendredis has been built this way: one Sunday at a time.
