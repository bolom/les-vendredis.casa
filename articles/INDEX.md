## KEY THEMES ACROSS ALL ARTICLES

**Craft & Competence**
Consistent message: excellence emerges from skill, knowledge, and commitment to doing things well. Whether shingling, scaffolding, or garden design, visible quality indicates someone who understands their work.

**Integration Not Domination**
The building exists within landscape, not against it. Materials are local. Design respects existing conditions. Infrastructure is hidden. The philosophy throughout is harmony rather than conquest.

**Labor as Dignity**
The work of building is portrayed with respect. Whether high-skill trades or landscape maintenance, labor is acknowledged as essential, valuable, and worthy of pride.

**Ecological Responsibility**
Sustainable choices emerge from practical necessity and economic sense, reinforced by environmental ethics. Salvage, local materials, minimal disturbance—these are both smart and right.

**Temporal Documentation**
Progress photographs capture moments that won't exist once construction is complete. This documentation preserves the process, honoring the work that becomes invisible in the finished product.

**Human Connection**
Throughout: the people doing the work, the families inhabiting the space, the community supporting development. Buildings aren't abstract—they're made by and for humans, within relationships.
