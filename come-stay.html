What to know before you come.

The real place, not the brochure.

A family home, not a hotel

Les Vendredis is our home in Sainte-Luce. We garden here, build here, eat outside here. When we're away, we open it to people who love simple, lived-in places.

Human welcome

No cold autonomous check-in. When the house is available, we welcome guests ourselves or arrange a real human contact. You arrive knowing someone.

Samsam and Dimdim

Samsam, our Creole dog, is here depending on the period. Dimdim, the rooster, is part of the soundscape. The garden, the heat, the sounds, the insects — they are part of the place.

Simple and imperfect

If you're looking for a smooth, standardised place, this is probably not it. The house is simple, alive, and imperfect. Photos are real, not staged to sell.

How to book.

Direct is simpler. Platforms also available.

Write to Bolo

The simplest way. Tell me your dates, I'll confirm availability and we can talk about the stay. Direct price from 80 €/night, minimum 2 nights.

Via Airbnb or Booking

You can also book on Airbnb or Booking if you prefer. Platforms sometimes apply their own fees or promotions — the direct price is always the reference.

Avis disponibles

Reviews from previous guests are visible on Airbnb and Booking. You can also write to me before booking to ask anything you want to know.
