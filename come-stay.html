What to know
before you come.

A family home, not a hotel

Les Vendredis is our home in Sainte-Luce. We garden here, build here, eat outside here. When we're away, we open it to people who love simple, lived-in places.

Human welcome

No cold autonomous check-in. When the house is available, we welcome guests ourselves or arrange a real human contact.

Samsam and Dimdim

Samsam, our Creole dog, is here depending on the period. Dimdim, the rooster, is part of the soundscape. The garden, the heat, the sounds — they are part of the place.

Simple and imperfect

If you're looking for a smooth, standardised place, this is probably not it. Photos are real, not staged to sell. Airbnb and Booking reviews are available when they exist.

Direct price from 80 €/night, minimum 2 nights. You can also book on Airbnb or Booking if you prefer — but the simplest thing is to write to me.
