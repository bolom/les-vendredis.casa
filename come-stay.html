Les Vendredis isn't a hotel. It's our home in Sainte-Luce. We garden here, build here, eat outside here, and when we're away we open it to people who love simple, lived-in places.

There's Samsam, our Creole dog, here depending on the period. There's Dimdim, the rooster, in the soundscape. There's the garden, the heat, the sounds, the insects. The house is alive and imperfect. Photos on Airbnb and Booking are real, not staged to sell.

No cold autonomous check-in. When the house is available, we welcome you ourselves, or we arrange a real human contact.

If that's the kind of place you're looking for, write to me.
