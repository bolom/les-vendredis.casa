The A-frame · Sainte-Luce, Martinique

Come stay
with us.

Les Vendredis is our family home in Martinique. When we're not there, you can come and sleep here.

This is not a hotel. Not a resort. There is Samsam, our Creole dog. There is Dimdim, the rooster. There is the garden, the heat, the sounds, the things that are not always perfect.

If you're looking for a smooth, standardised place, this is probably not it. If you want to step into our world for a few days, write to me.

Write to Bolo → Airbnb → Booking.com →

The direct price starts at 80 €/night. Platforms sometimes apply their own fees or promotions. If you've read the journal and think this place suits you, the simplest thing is to write to me.

What to know before you come

  • This is our family home, not a hotel.
  • Human welcome: no cold autonomous check-in. When the house is available, we welcome guests ourselves or arrange a real human contact.
  • Samsam, our Creole dog, is here depending on the period.
  • Dimdim, the rooster, is part of the soundscape.
  • The house is simple, alive, imperfect.
  • The garden, the sounds, the heat, the insects — they are part of the place.
  • Direct price from 80 €/night.
  • Minimum stay: 2 nights.
  • Real photos, not staged to sell.
  • Airbnb and Booking reviews available when they exist.
  • Direct contact with Bolo possible before booking.

You can book via Airbnb or Booking if you prefer. But the simplest thing is to write to me.
