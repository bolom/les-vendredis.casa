Our home
is open
when we’re
not there.

— Bolo

This is not a hotel.

It’s our home in Sainte-Luce. We garden here, build here, eat outside here. When we’re away, we open it to people who love simple, lived-in places.

There’s Samsam, our Creole dog, here depending on the period. There’s Dimdim, the rooster, in the soundscape. There’s the garden, the heat, the sounds, the insects.

The house is alive and imperfect. The photos here are real, not staged to sell.

No cold autonomous check-in. When the house is available, we welcome you ourselves, or we arrange a real human contact.

If that sounds like your kind of place, write to me.
